Kranti transport prototype: server is running

The server is running.

This is the small back end for the Kranti transport (Gold) prototype. The transporter's app is the separate Flutter app. These pages are what the requester and the other party see on their phones.

Open the demo phone inbox   About and plans

Try it in 6 steps

  1. In the Flutter app, log in and press Create. Choose a requester and Load or Unload, then send.
  2. Open the demo phone inbox. Click the link in the new message. That is the requester's page. Fill it in.
  3. Back in the app, the alert pops up. Accept the request.
  4. Open the order, add driver and vehicle, then press Send for confirmation.
  5. In the inbox, click the new link. That is the other party's page. Press Get my code.
  6. The code arrives in the inbox. Type it into the order in the app. The receipt is sealed.

Demo only: SMS is a fake inbox, email is only recorded, and there is no PDF, QR or anchor.
