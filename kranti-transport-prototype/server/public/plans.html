Kranti: about and plans

One idea: a sealed receipt of a transaction

Kranti turns an informal deal into a sealed, checkable receipt. The deal is recorded, the other party confirms it from a link, and at the handover a one-time code is read out and entered.

Diamond

For sellers of goods. Web-first. Sealed receipt: IAR.

Gold

For transporters. Mobile-first with app alerts. Sealed receipt: GDRN. This is the subscription this prototype shows.

Platinum

Custom requests, handled by our team.

This page stands in for the separate web page where plans are shown and bought. There is no payment, no in-app purchase and no pricing in the prototype.
